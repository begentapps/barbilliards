# Bar Billiards: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 26 November 2026*

**Bar Billiards does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It does not connect to the internet.**

This page explains exactly what the game keeps on your device and what it doesn't do.

## What the game keeps on your device

The game saves a few things so it remembers you between plays. They are stored only on your device, in the game's own private storage, and are never sent anywhere.

| What | Why | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Your settings | So the game looks and sounds the way you chose | Cloth colour, sound on/off, game length, brightness |
| High scores | Your best games, shown on the high score boards | Score, best break, date, game length, number of shots |
| Your stats | The "Your stats" page in the menu | Games played, balls potted by hole, fouls, results against the computer |
| Tutorial progress | So the game doesn't keep offering the tutorial | Whether you've been offered it |

None of this identifies you. The game never asks for your name, email or any account. High scores are labelled only "Player 1", "Player 2" or by the computer's level (for example "vs Champion").

**To delete it all**, uninstall the game. On Android you can also go to **Settings → Apps → Bar Billiards → Storage → Clear data**.
## What the game does not do

- **No internet.** The Android version does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send or receive anything.
- **No accounts or sign-in.** There is nothing to register for.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The game doesn't measure how you play or report it to anyone, including the developer and Unity, the company that makes the game engine. All of Unity's reporting services (analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics, performance reporting and hardware statistics) are switched off.
- **No advertising** and no advertising identifiers.
- **No third-party code** that collects data. There are no ad, analytics or social networking libraries in the game.
- **No device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.** The game doesn't ask for any of them.
- **No in-app purchases** at present. If purchases are added later, they will be handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and this page will be updated first.

## Permissions

The Android version asks for one permission:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Vibrate | A short buzz when you pot a ball. You can switch it off in **Settings → Sound**. |

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects none from children.

## App store privacy labels

- **Google Play Data safety:** No data collected. No data shared.
- **Apple App Store privacy label:** Data Not Collected.

## Changes to this policy

If anything here ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version of the game is released, and the date at the top will change.
