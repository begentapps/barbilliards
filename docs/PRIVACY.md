# Bar Billiards: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 26 November 2026*

**Bar Billiards is free, with occasional ads between games, and a one-off purchase removes them.**
**The game itself collects nothing about you. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads, as explained below. If you buy "Remove ads", no ads are requested at all.**

## What the game keeps on your device

The game saves a few things so it remembers you between plays. They are stored only on your device, in the game's own private storage, and are never sent anywhere.

| What | Why | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Your settings | So the game looks and sounds the way you chose | Cloth colour, sound on/off, game length, brightness |
| High scores | Your best games, shown on the high score boards | Score, best break, date, game length, number of shots |
| Your stats | The "Your stats" page in the menu | Games played, balls potted by hole, fouls, results against the computer |
| Tutorial progress | So the game doesn't keep offering the tutorial | Whether you've been offered it |
| Ads | So ads start only after your first few games, and stop if you've removed them | Number of games finished, whether you've bought Remove ads |

None of this identifies you. The game never asks for your name, email or any account. High scores are labelled only "Player 1", "Player 2" or by the computer's level (for example "vs Champion").

**To delete it all**, uninstall the game. On Android you can also go to **Settings → Apps → Bar Billiards → Storage → Clear data**. (A Remove ads purchase is kept by Google Play and comes back when you reinstall.)

## Ads (Google AdMob)

Your first three games have no ads, and the ad software isn't even started. After that, a full-screen ad may appear between games (never while you're playing), at most once every five minutes.

The ads come from **Google AdMob**. When the game asks for an ad, Google's ad software collects and shares with Google, according to [Google's own disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure):

- your device's **IP address**, which may be used to estimate your general location;
- **interactions with ads**, such as app launches, taps and video views;
- **diagnostic information**, such as app launch time and how the ad software is performing;
- **device identifiers**: Android's advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is encrypted in transit. How Google uses it is set out in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and on [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Your choices:**

- **In the EU, UK, Switzerland and places with similar laws**, Google asks for your consent before the first ad, and you can choose not to consent. You can change your choice at any time in the game: **Menu → Remove ads → Ad privacy choices**.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete it in your device's settings (on Android: **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).
- **Ads suitable for all**: the game asks Google only for ads rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance.
- **Remove ads:** after the one-off purchase, the game never requests an ad again.

## In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-off purchase made through **Google Play** (or the Apple App Store on iPhone). Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple: the game never sees your payment details. The game only asks the store whether your account owns the purchase, so it can switch the ads off, including after a reinstall or on a new device.

## What the game does not do

- **No accounts or sign-in.** There is nothing to register for.
- **No analytics or tracking by the game or by Unity.** The game doesn't measure how you play or report it to anyone, including the developer and Unity, the company that makes the game engine. All of Unity's reporting services (analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics, performance reporting and hardware statistics) are switched off. Apart from Google's ad software described above, there are no analytics or social networking libraries in the game.
- **No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.** The game doesn't ask for any of them.
- **The developer receives no data about you.** AdMob gives the developer only overall totals, such as how many ads were shown.

## Permissions

The Android version asks for:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet and network state | To fetch ads, and Google's consent message |
| Advertising ID, and Android's ad services ("Privacy Sandbox") | Added by Google's ad software (see above) |
| Billing | For the Remove ads purchase through Google Play |
| Keep awake, background service | Added by Google's libraries for their own background tasks; the game doesn't use them |
| Vibrate | A short buzz when you pot a ball. You can switch it off in **Settings → Sound**. |

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the ads aren't targeted at children. The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children; it collects none itself.
